Kairox Studios / Careers

Build worlds with us.

Small team. Serious work. Games that earn the time players spend on them. If that sentence resonates, you might belong here.

Why Kairox

A studio where your work ships with your name on it.

01

No crunch, ever

We scope projects to match our team size. When timelines slip, we adjust scope, not schedules. The people who built this studio came from environments where crunch was normalized, and they started Kairox specifically to change that.

02

Full credits, always

Every contributor is credited in every game they worked on. No contractor exceptions. No contractor-versus-full-time distinctions in the credits roll. If you built something, your name is on it.

03

Ownership over titles

We do not have a hierarchy of titles. We have responsibilities. Every person on a project has a clearly defined area of ownership and the authority to make decisions within it without approvals from above.

04

Tools that match the ambition

We built our own engine because nothing else was right for what we make. We spend on good tools. We invest in proper hardware. We do not ask people to produce excellent work on inadequate infrastructure.

05

Games that need you

The kind of games we make are not possible at scale. They need people who are invested in them as creative objects, not as SKUs. If you want to work on something that genuinely requires your expertise, you will find it here.

Compensation and Benefits

What comes with the role

PAY

Competitive Salary

Benchmarked to senior-tier ranges in EU and NA markets. We pay the same regardless of where you are located. No location-adjusted pay.

EQUITY

Profit Sharing

A meaningful percentage of net revenue from titles you ship is distributed to the team. The better the games do, the better you do.

TIME

Flexible Hours

No core hours beyond a 4-hour overlap window. Work when your thinking is sharpest. Results are the measure, not presence.

HEALTH

Healthcare Coverage

Full medical, dental, and vision for you and dependents. For regions where public coverage applies, we provide a supplemental private plan.

LEAVE

Unlimited PTO

No PTO tracking. Leadership models taking real time off. We have a suggested minimum of 25 days annually because we want people rested, not depleted.

SETUP

Hardware Budget

Full workstation budget on day one, refreshed every three years. We include a peripheral and software budget for ongoing setup upgrades.

GROW

Learning Budget

Annual stipend for courses, conferences, and books. No approval required below the threshold. Attend the conference. Buy the thing that makes you better at your work.

CREDIT

Full Game Credits

Your name in the credits of every game you contributed to. Permanent, non-negotiable, applies to contractors and full-time staff equally.

Hiring Process

01

Application

Response within 5 business days

Intro Call

A 30-minute conversation with someone from the relevant team. Not a formal interview. We want to understand your work and you want to understand ours. Mutual fit.

30 minutes
03

Skills Review

A paid, bounded task relevant to the role. One week maximum. We review and discuss it together. We do not keep the work. You keep the fee regardless of outcome.

Paid / 1 week max
04

Team Interview

A 90-minute session with 3 to 4 people you would work with directly. Focused on collaboration and judgment, not trivia. An offer follows within 48 hours if it is right.

90 minutes / Decision in 48 hrs
