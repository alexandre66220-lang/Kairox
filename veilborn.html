Veilborn

"Every choice echoes in eternity."

You are Lysara, Oracle of the Threshold. The gods have gone silent. The dead refuse their rest. What you divine shapes not just your fate, but the fate of every soul caught between worlds.

The Oracle

"The threshold is not a place. It is a moment between heartbeats."

Greece, an age the gods abandoned. Temples crumble. Oracles fall silent. But Lysara still hears them: the voices of the restless dead, the whispers of forgotten prophecy, the sound of choices not yet made.

To restore balance between the living and the realm below, she must descend. Every vision she receives is both gift and burden. Every soul she judges becomes part of her.

Protagonist

Lysara

Oracle of the Threshold. Daughter of a dying priesthood. She carries the weight of divine sight in a world that no longer believes in it.

The Underworld

A World Between Worlds

Mechanics

Built for the Solitary Journey

01

Oracle Sight

Activate vision to see the true nature of a soul, place, or moment. Each vision costs something. Use it without thought and you will run dry at the worst possible moment.

02

Judgment System

Every soul Lysara encounters carries a history. You decide what they deserve. Your choices ripple through the world above: weather, allegiances, the shape of prophecy itself.

03

Memory Weave

Collect fragments of the dead's memories. Piece them together to unlock hidden truths, new paths, or the ability to alter what has already happened. Some truths cost more than others.

04

Cinematic Narrative

No combat systems. No experience points. Veilborn is a pure narrative action experience. Every scene, every confrontation, is authored and directed. The player shapes the story, not the numbers.

05

Consequence Architecture

Decisions made in hour one reshape what is possible in hour twelve. There are no morality meters. The world simply becomes what Lysara's choices have made it, without judgment.
